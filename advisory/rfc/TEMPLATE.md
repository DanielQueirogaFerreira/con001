# RFC NNN — Title

## The claim under review

State exactly what the system would assert, in the system's own closed
vocabulary (`answers-similarly`, `answers-oppositely`, `shares-question-only`),
and quote the axis it sits on.

## The text it rests on

Every anchored interpretation cited as evidence, with its Canonical Reference,
so a reviewer can read the passage rather than the summary.

## The recorded divergence

What the claim says these readings do **not** share. Required on every
resonance, including agreements.

## What each seat is asked to decide

One numbered question per seat. Not "do you approve" — a specific question a
scholar of that tradition can answer from within it.

## What a reviewer may do

- `approve` — the claim is a fair statement of this tradition's position.
- `approve-with-changes` — fair once the noted changes are made. Record them.
- `object` — not a fair statement. An objection blocks approval and stays on the
  record. It is not overruled by other seats.

An objection is not a veto over the *other* tradition's reading. It is a veto
over what this system says about **your own**.
